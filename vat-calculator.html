Frequently Asked Questions
What VAT rates are included for 2026?▼
All 27 EU member states, UK (standard 20%, reduced 5%), Norway (25%) and Switzerland (8.1%). Standard, reduced and super-reduced rates are all included, updated for 2026.
How do I remove VAT from a gross price?▼
Select 'Remove VAT' mode, enter the gross (VAT-inclusive) amount, choose your country and rate type. The tool extracts the net price and VAT component separately.
Is the VAT Calculator completely free?▼
Yes — completely free. No sign-up, no account, no data stored. It runs entirely in your browser.
Can I use this for B2B reverse-charge transactions?▼
The calculator shows net and gross amounts. For reverse-charge scenarios, the net price is what you invoice to a registered business in another EU member state. Confirm the specific rules with your tax adviser.
How often are VAT rates updated?▼
We review and update rates at the start of each calendar year and whenever the EU Commission announces changes.
